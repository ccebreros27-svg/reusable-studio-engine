Tool Intent Statement

Core Function

This tool interrupts compulsive phone opening with a short pause ritual. It does not block access; it inserts awareness between impulse and action.

Human Context

It enters the moment of reflex phone checking: boredom scrolling, stress avoidance, and repeat unlock loops where the body moves before attention catches up.

Intended User

A student or young adult who unlocks their phone dozens of times a day and wants to reclaim choice without being shamed or monitored.

Behavioral Thesis

Behavior change begins with interruption. Friction restores agency. Awareness precedes control. A short pause can reduce autopilot and return ownership of attention.

Dignity Clause

No tracking, no permanent storage, no scores, no streaks, no surveillance framing. The interface avoids urgency, guilt, and manipulative reward cues.

Refusal Clause

This will not become a productivity dashboard, app blocker, self-discipline engine, dopamine replacement, or behavior monitoring system.

Atmospheric Tone

Calm, minimal, and slow—like stepping into quiet air before unlocking a screen. The pause should feel like a breath, not a warning.
